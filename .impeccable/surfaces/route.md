# Surface brief — `/` (signed-out landing)

**Scope:** Signed-out landing page at `/`. Signed-in users see the Decks home
(separate brief, Operate mode).

**Visitor mode:** Persuade.

**Audience:** Self-directed learners studying technical or layered subjects at a
computer, in a focused session. Technically literate; respect their attention.

**Job / action:** Understand what Armin actually does — knowledge as a graph,
cards locked behind prerequisites, FSRS-spaced review — and start studying free
(sign up / sign in).

**Direction:** Field Notes editorial structure, assigned by surface concept seed
`6f7dcf54` (candidate 5 of the grounded list). The page reads as a short study
note — "How durable knowledge is built" — where each section demonstrates the
mechanism rather than claiming it: a live prerequisite graph showing a card
locked until its foundations are learned, a due stack, the FSRS rating grid in
action. Serif-led, quiet, content-forward, inside the established Flexoki
ink-on-paper world (DESIGN.md). Sign-up anchored at first viewport and at the
close.

**Constraints:** Established Flexoki world — no new palette/type identity. Warm
and grown-up, never gamified; no decorative motion, no gradient text, no
hero-metric template, no kicker/eyebrow labels. Pricing stays factual and
subordinate ($5/mo AI plan per ADR 0005). Keyboard-navigable, WCAG 2.1 AA.

**Memorable moment:** The prerequisite graph at work — a dependent flashcard
staying locked until its foundations are learned, visible on the page.

**Unresolved:** Copy drafts are authorable; product claims stay limited to what
PRODUCT.md/ADR 0005 state. Dark mode applies once the ramp exists.
