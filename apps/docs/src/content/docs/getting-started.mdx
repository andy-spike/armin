  order: 1
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Open [armincards.app](https://armincards.app) and sign up with your email or Google account.
    Verify your email before you add cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a deck">
    Choose **Decks** in the sidebar and create one. A deck holds cards and the prerequisite links
    between them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add cards">
    Add a few **Basic** cards: a question on the front, the answer on the back. See [Cards and
    decks](/guides/cards-and-decks/) for other types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Choose **Review**, recall the answer, reveal it, and rate how well you remembered. See
    [Reviewing](/guides/reviewing/).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Already use Anki? [Import your decks](/guides/import-from-anki/).
