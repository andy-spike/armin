This page takes you from a new account to your first finished review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Open [armincards.app](https://armincards.app) and sign up with your email address or Google
    account. Verify your email address before you add cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a deck">
    A deck holds a set of cards and the prerequisite links between them. Create one from **Decks**
    in the sidebar and give it a name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add cards">
    Open the deck and add a few cards. Start with **Basic** cards: a question on the front and its
    answer on the back. See [Cards and decks](/guides/cards-and-decks/) for the other types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Choose **Review**. Recall the answer, reveal it, and rate how well you remembered it. Armin
    schedules the next review from your rating. See [Reviewing](/guides/reviewing/).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Already use Anki?

You can import your Anki decks, including their review history, instead of
starting from scratch. See [Import from Anki](/guides/import-from-anki/).
