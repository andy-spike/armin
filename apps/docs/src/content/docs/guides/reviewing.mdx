A review shows one card at a time. Recall the answer, reveal it, then rate how well you remembered.

## Ratings

| Rating | Use it when                                    |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Again  | You didn't remember. The card comes back soon. |
| Hard   | You remembered, with real effort.              |
| Good   | You remembered with normal effort.             |
| Easy   | You remembered instantly.                      |

Armin schedules the next review with [FSRS](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/fsrs4anki/wiki), and each button shows when you'd see the card next. **Undo** restores the card to how it was before your last rating.

## What each day contains

Each day's queue holds due cards, oldest first, then new cards. Two settings under **Settings → Learning path** limit it across all your decks:

- **New review units per day** caps new material.
- **Reviews per day** caps new and due cards together. It's empty (no limit) by default.

A card already on screen can always be answered, even if a limit is reached mid-session.

## Cards you unlock while reviewing

When a rating secures a [prerequisite](/guides/prerequisites/), Armin tells you how many cards it unlocked. They don't join the current session. When you finish, choose **Study _n_ unlocked cards** to review them as a new round, or leave them for later. They count toward **New review units per day**, so once that limit is reached the rest arrive on a later day.

Other cards can still join a session in progress, such as one that becomes due or one you add in another tab. A **+** count next to the session total shows how many joined.

## Bury

**Bury until tomorrow** hides every review unit of a card until your next study day without changing its schedule. Undo reverses it.

## Cram

**Cram** lets you practice a deck outside the schedule, for example before an exam. Cram ratings don't change any schedule or review history. They count toward your daily activity and streak on **Stats**, but not toward retention.
