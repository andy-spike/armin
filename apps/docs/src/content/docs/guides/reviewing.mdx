A review shows you one card at a time. Try to recall the answer, reveal it,
then rate how well you remembered.

## Ratings

| Rating | Use it when                                    |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Again  | You didn't remember. The card comes back soon. |
| Hard   | You remembered, with real effort.              |
| Good   | You remembered with normal effort.             |
| Easy   | You remembered instantly.                      |

Armin schedules the next review with [FSRS](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/fsrs4anki/wiki),
which models how quickly you forget each card. Each rating button shows when
you would see the card next.

Made a mistake? **Undo** restores the card to exactly how it was before your
last rating.

## What each day contains

Each day's queue holds cards that are due, oldest first, followed by new cards.
Two settings under **Settings → Learning path** limit it, across all your
decks:

- **New review units per day** caps how much new material enters study each
  day.
- **Reviews per day** caps the total, new and due together. It's empty, meaning
  no limit, by default.

A card that's already on screen can always be answered, even if a limit is
reached mid-session.

## Bury

**Bury until tomorrow** hides every review unit of a card until the start of your next study
day. Its schedule doesn't change, and the card returns on its own. Undo
reverses a bury.

## Cram

**Cram** lets you practise a deck outside the schedule, for example before an
exam. Cram ratings don't change any schedule or review history. They do count
toward your daily activity and streak on **Stats**, but not toward retention.
