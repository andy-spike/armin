Armin works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Claude Code, Codex, and other MCP
clients. Open **AI setup** in the sidebar for step-by-step instructions for each
one.

## What an assistant can do

After you approve a connection, the assistant can work across your whole
library:

- Read, create, edit, archive, move, and link cards.
- Create and rename decks.
- [Import Anki files](/guides/import-from-anki/) with a preview you approve.

It **can't** permanently delete anything, rate reviews, change scheduling,
export your library, or manage your account.

## Manage connections

**Settings → AI assistants** lists every connection. Disconnect one at any time
and its access ends immediately.

For technical details, see the [MCP server
documentation](https://armincards.app/docs/mcp).
