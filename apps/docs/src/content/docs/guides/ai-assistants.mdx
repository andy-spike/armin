Armin works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Claude Code, Codex, and other MCP
clients. Open **AI setup** in the sidebar for step-by-step instructions for each
one.

## What an assistant can do

Each connection asks for one or more permission groups, and each group covers
your whole library:

- **Read**: find and read decks, cards, prerequisite links, study stats, and card
  details.
- **Write**: create and edit cards and decks, archive cards, move cards, and link
  prerequisites.
- **Import**: [convert Anki files](/guides/import-from-anki/) into new decks with
  a preview you approve.
- **Study**: show cards that are due and record your own ratings.

A client that asks for no permission receives all four. An assistant with the
study group shows one prompt at a time, hides the answer until you answer, and
records only the rating you give; it never picks a rating for you.

It **can't** permanently delete anything, reschedule cards on its own, export
your library, or manage your account.

## Manage connections

**Settings → AI assistants** lists every connection and the permission groups it
has. Disconnect one at any time and its access ends immediately.

Connections approved before the permission groups existed are marked as needing
reconnection. Reconnect from your assistant and approve the current groups to
continue.

For technical details, see the [MCP server
documentation](https://armincards.app/docs/mcp).
