Armin works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Claude Code, Codex, and other MCP clients. Open **AI setup** in the sidebar for steps for each.

## What an assistant can do

Each connection asks for permission groups, and each group covers your whole library:

- **Read**: find and read decks, cards, prerequisite links, and study stats.
- **Write**: create and edit cards and decks, archive and move cards, and link prerequisites.
- **Import**: [convert Anki files](/guides/import-from-anki/) into new decks, with a preview you approve.
- **Study**: show due cards one at a time, hide the answer until you reply, and record only the rating you give.

An assistant can't permanently delete anything, reschedule cards, export your library, or manage your account.

## Manage connections

**Settings → AI assistants** lists each connection and its permission groups. Disconnect one and its access ends immediately.

See the [MCP server reference](/reference/mcp-server/) for details.
