Open **Import from Anki** on the deck list, or **Settings → Data → Import**.
Armin offers two ways in.

<Tabs syncKey="anki-import">
  <TabItem label="With an AI assistant">
    Recommended for large or image-heavy decks. Give the Anki file to a
    [connected assistant](/guides/ai-assistants/) with file access. It runs
    Armin's converter, reports anything it couldn't convert, and can repair or
    skip those cards on request. It shows you a final preview and saves only
    after you approve.

    Assistant imports always start fresh: no Anki due dates or review history.

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="In the browser">
    Choose your file and Armin reads it in your browser. The preview lists
    anything that won't come across before you import.

    Supported files are Anki decks (`.apkg`), whole collections (`.colpkg`),
    and plain-text exports (`.txt`, `.csv`, `.tsv`). You can optionally bring
    your review progress along.

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What comes across

- Each Anki deck becomes an Armin deck.
- Basic, reversed, typed-answer, cloze, and image occlusion notes become the
  matching Armin card types. Other note types are rendered from their
  templates, or you choose which fields go on each side.
- Notes from the older Image Occlusion Enhanced add-on become image occlusion
  cards, one per image. Other occlusion formats, such as AnKing's "IO-one by
  one", are listed as not imported.
- Images (SVG becomes PNG), audio, formatting, flags (as `flag::<color>`
  tags), and deck options such as desired retention and FSRS parameters.
- Tags up to 200 characters and 100 per card, including hierarchical tags such
  as `#AK_Step1_v12::Pharm`.

Video, text-to-speech, card styling, and per-deck daily limits don't come
across.

## Large decks

A deck holds up to 10,000 cards, so a bigger Anki deck imports as numbered
decks such as `AnKing (1 of 4)`, keeping each note's cards together.

If the file doesn't fit your plan, the preview offers to import the first cards
that fit: reviewed cards by due date, then new cards in Anki's order. Upgrade
later and import the file again for the rest.
