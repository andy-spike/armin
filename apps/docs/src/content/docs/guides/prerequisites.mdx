A card can have **prerequisites**: other cards in the same deck you should know first. These links form the deck's **prerequisite graph**.

## Locked cards

A card stays **locked**, and out of reviews, while any prerequisite isn't **secured**. A prerequisite is secured once every one of its review units has graduated to review and reached the **Prerequisite stability** set under **Settings → Learning path**.

Once unlocked, a card stays unlocked. If you later forget a prerequisite, Armin keeps scheduling it for practice, but the cards that depend on it remain available.

<Aside type="note">
  Imported cards have no prerequisites, so they are never locked. Add links after importing if you
  want them.
</Aside>

## Linking cards

Open a deck's **Graph** to see its cards and links and to add or remove prerequisites. Armin rejects links that would form a cycle.

Keep prerequisites small and specific, and use them only where order matters.
