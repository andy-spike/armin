# One free tier and one $5/month paid plan, AI-only

Armin has exactly two offerings: a **free tier** and one **paid plan at $5/month
(USD)**. The free tier includes every study feature with no feature gating — only
a media-storage quota bounds it. The paid plan adds only the AI features (card
generation, AI explanations, card improvement) behind a bundled monthly AI
allowance. There is no free trial: the free tier is the whole onboarding path.

The price is deliberately low because the free tier is genuinely complete; Users
pay to unlock AI, nothing else. Billing runs through Polar.sh. Adding a second
paid tier later would be a documented reversal of the "AI-only" positioning.
