Armin's code is open source under the [MIT](LICENSE). The license covers
the code only. It does not grant rights to the **Armin** name, the Armin logo
and mark, or the `armincards.app` domain, which identify the service operated
by Andy Spike.

You may, without asking:

- Run, modify, and share the code under the MIT.
- Self-host Armin for your own use and keep calling it Armin there.
- Say truthfully that your project is "based on Armin" or "a fork of Armin".
- Use the name to refer to Armin, for example in an article, review, or
  integration ("works with Armin").

Please don't, without written permission:

- Offer a modified or unmodified Armin to other people under the Armin name or
  logo, or under a name or logo confusingly similar to them. A public fork or
  hosted service needs its own name and logo.
- Suggest that your project, service, or product is official, endorsed by, or
  affiliated with Armin.
- Use the name or logo in a domain, app-store listing, or account name for
  something other than the official service.

Questions or permission requests: **support@armincards.app**.
